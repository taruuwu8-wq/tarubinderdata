เลเวลถัดไป: [![](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/img/gen/map/wreckwake_coast.png)Wreckwake Coast](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/map/wreckwake_coast) Lv 99–102 · [![](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/img/gen/map/lanternrice_delta.png)Lanternrice Delta](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/map/lanternrice_delta) Lv 101–104สายไหนได้เปรียบ**สายกายภาพ** ได้เปรียบ — มอนต้านเวท 50% · Infusion ธาตุ Shadow 50% · Earth 50% ช่วยมอนตีก่อนไหม[![](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/img/mob/gloom_skitter.png)Gloom Skitter](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/mob/gloom_skitter) [![](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/img/mob/lantern_moth.png)Lantern Moth](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/mob/lantern_moth)Elite / บอส[![](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/img/mob/vale_warden.png)Vale Warden](https://wiki.bindertowngame.com/mob/vale_warden) Field BossChannel 10 (PvP)
